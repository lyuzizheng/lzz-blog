# Product

## Register

brand

## Users

Visitors who want to understand Zizheng Lyu as an engineer, maker, writer, and photographer. This includes peers, collaborators, hiring audiences, friends, and readers arriving for a specific article or project.

## Product Purpose

LZZ Blog is a personal digital atelier that brings engineering work, long-form writing, photography, independent products, and career history into one coherent public record. Success means a visitor can quickly grasp the person behind the work, then move naturally into deeper evidence without the site feeling like a generic portfolio or résumé.

## Brand Personality

Physical, precise, and curious. The voice should feel like a working darkroom and print studio: technically credible, visually expressive, personal without becoming sentimental, and playful without losing discipline.

## Anti-references

- Generic portfolio templates built from interchangeable cards, gradients, and oversized marketing copy.
- Polished SaaS landing pages that flatten personal work into product metrics or conversion funnels.
- Decorative retro styling that imitates film without useful hierarchy, material logic, or real content.
- Dense technical dashboards, neon terminal cosplay, glassmorphism, and motion that competes with reading.

## Design Principles

1. Show the work as evidence: let writing, photographs, projects, timelines, and records carry the story.
2. Treat every page as part of one atelier: preserve the darkroom and physical-print metaphor without forcing identical layouts.
3. Pair expressive moments with precise information: visual personality must improve orientation, hierarchy, and recall.
4. Keep the human and technical identities together: neither engineering nor life should become a decorative secondary track.
5. Prefer durable, public-safe records over promotional claims: clarity and trust matter more than volume.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Target WCAG 2.1 AA for contrast, semantics, keyboard access, and focus visibility. Respect reduced-motion preferences, keep essential content available without animation, avoid color-only meaning, preserve readable line lengths and touch targets, and maintain equivalent English and Simplified Chinese navigation and content structure.
